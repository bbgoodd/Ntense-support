Connecting your Ntense device

Turn on your Ntense device, keep it near your phone and allow Bluetooth access for Ntense. Open the app and choose Connect device. Live measurements and device settings require a compatible Ntense device.

Importing saved sessions

Save your Ntense JSON export in the Files app. In Ntense, open History, open the import/export menu, choose Import and select your file. A hardware connection is not required to review imported sessions.
