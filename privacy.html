Measurement and device data

Ntense processes force measurements received from a Ntense device over Bluetooth. Saved sessions may include measurement samples, dates, durations, statistics, tags and device information. The app also stores preferences and remembered device identifiers and names locally on your device to provide measurements, history and reconnection functionality.

Ntense does not operate a developer-hosted measurement database or require an app account. The app does not send your measurement history to the developer automatically. No advertising or analytics SDK is integrated.

Bluetooth and files

Bluetooth access is used to discover and communicate with your Ntense device. You can revoke access in iOS Settings; hardware-dependent features will then be unavailable. Import reads the JSON file you select. Export writes session data to the location you choose. If you choose iCloud Drive or another storage provider, that provider processes the file under its own terms and privacy policy.

Font downloads in build 4

The device information screen uses the Google Fonts package for Roboto Mono. If the font is not already cached, the app can request the font file from fonts.gstatic.com. This makes your IP address and technical request information available to Google. Measurement data and device identifiers are not included in the font request. The downloaded font is cached locally.

See Google’s privacy policy. This font request is separate from measurement storage.

Retention and deletion on your device

Saved sessions remain until you delete them in History or remove the app and its data. Remembered devices can be removed using the app’s device controls. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local app data. Device backups may retain copies according to your backup settings. Exported files remain wherever you saved or shared them and must be deleted separately. The developer cannot remotely access or delete data stored only on your device.

Support email

If you email support, your email address, message and any attachments are processed to answer your request. Support uses Gmail, so Google also processes these communications under its privacy terms. Support correspondence is deleted within six months after the request is resolved, unless a legal obligation requires longer retention. You can request earlier deletion by email, subject to applicable legal obligations.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no analytics scripts, advertising, external fonts or developer-set cookies. GitHub processes connection information, including IP addresses, to serve and secure the site. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Following an external link or sending email is subject to the destination service’s privacy practices.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data and to receive a portable copy. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Contact the email address above to exercise rights concerning data held by the developer. Local-only measurements are managed directly in the app and through its export function.
